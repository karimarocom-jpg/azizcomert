Privacy Policy

RestauAComert. — Effective date: November 10, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the RestauAComert. mobile application ("the App") handles information. We respect your privacy and designed the App to work without collecting any personal data.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information. Specifically:

  • The App does not require an account, login or registration.
  • The App does not collect your name, email address, phone number, payment details or any other personal data.
  • The App does not access your location. The "Open in Maps" button only opens your device's maps app with our restaurant's address.
  • The App does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.
  • The App does not use advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services.

3. QR codes

QR codes shown in the App are generated locally on your device. The App does not send any information when a QR code is displayed. If you scan a QR code with another device, that device may open a web page on our website, which is outside the App.

4. Language preference

Your language choice (English or Swedish) is used only while the App is open. It is not saved or shared.

5. Email

The "Email us" button opens your device's email app with our address filled in. Nothing is sent by the App itself. If you choose to email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you, and we do not share them with third parties.

6. Third-party services

The App does not include third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics or data collection. When you tap a button that opens another app (for example a maps or email app), that app's own privacy policy applies.

7. Children's privacy

The App is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU).

8. Data security and retention

Because we do not collect or store personal data, there is no personal data to protect, retain or delete.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you have rights over your personal data. Since the App holds none, there is nothing to access, correct or delete. If you have emailed us and want your message deleted, contact us and we will remove it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at the link on our Google Play listing, with the effective date updated above.
