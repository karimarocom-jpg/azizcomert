About Us

A Stockholm restaurant business since 2016

Honest food, friendly service, a place to feel welcome.

Aziz Cömert AB is a Swedish limited company (aktiebolag), registered in August 2016 and based at Spelvägen 5 in Trångsund, Stockholm. Our registered business is to operate a restaurant and conduct related activities.

We are a small, dedicated team focused on quality food and attentive service for our guests. Our mobile apps do not collect personal data and do not use tracking or analytics, and we are open about who we are and how to reach us.
